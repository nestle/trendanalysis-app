# REPORT ON EU AGR-FOOD FRAUD SUSPICONS
- The monthly report aims to assist Member States' authorities in enforcing regulations and providing selected information to stakeholders in the agri-food sector. 
- The report includes non-compliances with fraud suspicions related to cross-border nature and covers various aspects of the agri-food chain, such as food, feeding materials, animal welfare, plant protection products, and veterinary medicinal products.
- It does not reconcile data on subjects like animal and plant health, release into the environment of Genetically Modified Organisms, and animal by-products not intended for oral feeding to animals.
- The report defines fraud notification as non-compliance concerning any suspected intentional action violating regulations for the purpose of deceiving purchasers.
- Suspicions per product and fraud category, as well as the source of detection, are provided in the report.
- It also includes details on temporary increases of official border controls and emergency measures governing the entry into the Union of certain goods from third countries.

This report aims to combat fraudulent and deceptive practices in the EU agri-food chain by providing useful information to stakeholders and assisting Member States' authorities in enforcing regulations.
